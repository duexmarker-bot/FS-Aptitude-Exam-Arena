Quick-study playbook

Use these fast methods when the clock is running.

Coding & Decoding

Look for consistent letter shifts, reversals, substitutions, position values, or word transformations.

Number Series

Check first differences, second differences, ratios, alternating patterns, and changing multipliers.

Letter Series & Alpha-Numeric Series

Track alphabet positions separately from numbers. Test increasing or decreasing jumps and alternating rules.

Ranking, Ordering, Seating Arrangements & Puzzles

Translate every clue into an order or position. Build a small table before answering.

Number and Letter Analogies

Find the exact transformation from the first pair, then apply the same transformation to the second pair.

Verbal Analogies

Identify the relationship, not just the meaning of the individual words.

Direction Sense

Sketch a tiny compass and track every turn. Net horizontal and vertical movement often simplifies the problem.

Blood Relations

Build a family tree from the statement instead of trying to hold every relationship in your head.

Source audit

This app preserves the question text and answer key supplied in your PDF. A few entries in the PDF are visibly incomplete or internally mismatched, so they are flagged instead of being silently “fixed.”

  • Number and Letter Analogies Q21–Q32: the PDF references these questions, but their question text/options are not present in the supplied source. They are not invented here.
  • Flagged mismatches: Letter Series Q12, Number and Letter Analogies Q16, Q17, Q19, and Direction Sense Q2 contain answer/options inconsistencies in the supplied source. They are kept in the bank but excluded from scoring.
  • Context gaps: some ranking/seating and family-group questions lack their supporting arrangement/diagram in the supplied pages. Those remain playable using the source answer key, but the gap is noted when you review them.
